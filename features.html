Platform Features

Everything in one platform

Sector-based AI predictions, real-time dashboards, portfolio trading, expert applications, and platform-wide financial oversight, tailored by user role.

Core Trading Features
Real-Time Stock Dashboard
Live prices, candlestick charts, and trading volume across S&P 500 stocks. Search any tracked ticker and monitor price movements instantly.
Basic (limited) · Premium (unlimited)
Sector-Based AI Prediction
Eleven independent XGBoost models, one per S&P 500 sector, predict next-day stock movement from technical indicators. Each sector is calibrated and quality-gated separately, so weak signals are flagged as WATCH instead of a false BUY/SELL call.
Basic (limited) · Premium (unlimited)
Quant Sector Ranking
Part of the sector prediction feature: ranks every stock inside a sector by model confidence, letting users compare opportunities within the same industry at a glance instead of checking tickers one by one.
Basic (limited) · Premium (unlimited)
Watchlist Management
Add, view, monitor, and remove stocks from a personal watchlist. Real-time price updates keep shortlisted stocks always in view.
Basic + Premium
Portfolio Trading & Wallet
Buy and sell stocks against a platform wallet balance, with a small commission charged per trade. Deposits and withdrawals run through a dedicated Cash Portal, with withdrawals protected by a transaction PIN.
Basic + Premium
Risk Assessment Profiling
Identifies each user's risk tolerance to personalise stock recommendations and investment guidance, so advice matches their financial comfort zone.
Basic + Premium
Stock Recommendations
Buy, hold, or sell suggestions generated from sector prediction results and the user's risk profile, filtered and personalised per user.
Basic + Premium
Premium Features
Custom Stock Alerts
Set price-threshold or percentage-move alerts per stock, with an optional custom message. The system checks live prices continuously and emails a notification when a condition triggers.
Premium + Expert
Direct Messaging with Experts
Message verified experts directly for personalised investment guidance, instead of submitting a one-off consultation form. Conversations stay in one thread per expert.
Premium Only
Send Gifts to Experts
Send a monetary gift to an expert from within a conversation as a way to tip for helpful guidance, on top of any subscription fees already paid.
Premium Only
Gated Stock Discussion
A Premium-only forum category for deeper stock discussion, visible to Premium investors, verified experts, and admins only.
Premium Only
Expert Portfolios (Full Access)
View complete expert portfolios including stock selections, percentage allocations, performance graphs, and purchase rationale, with full details available to Premium subscribers only.
Premium Only
Learning & Community
Knowledge Hub
Educational articles and investment guides covering stock market concepts, trading strategies, and technical analysis. Basic users access limited content; Premium users unlock the full library.
Basic (limited) · Premium (full)
AI Chatbot
An intelligent chatbot providing instant stock insights, market analysis, and investment-related guidance. Basic users get a limited number of questions per day; Premium is unlimited.
Basic (limited) · Premium (unlimited)
Community Forum
Create, comment, and engage on trading discussions with other investors and experts. Share market ideas, ask questions, and stay updated on community insights.
All Users
Expert Portfolio Browsing
All users can browse the expert portfolio list. Basic users see limited summary info; Premium users unlock full strategy details, stock weightings, and performance metrics.
Basic (preview) · Premium (full)
Platform Reviews
Leave a star rating and written review of the platform itself. Reviews are public, one per user, and editable, with an admin removal-notice flow for content that breaks the guidelines.
All Users
Expert Features
Become an Expert
Investors can apply to become an Expert once they hit trading milestones (a minimum number of distinct stocks traded and a minimum profit margin), then submit a LinkedIn profile and supporting documents for review. There is no separate expert signup; the role is earned.
Investor → Expert
Create & Manage Expert Portfolios
Experts build curated stock portfolios with ticker allocations, percentage weights, and purchase rationale. Portfolios are published for investors to browse and learn from.
Expert
Message & Advise Investors
Reply to direct messages from Premium investors with professional investment guidance, strategy recommendations, and answers to enquiries.
Expert
Compensation Dashboard
A summary view of total earnings and the breakdown behind them, covering gifts from investors and the expert's share of related platform activity.
Expert
Finance Admin Features
Revenue & Cash Flow Dashboard
All-time revenue, revenue over a selected trailing window, net cash flow, and large-withdrawal monitoring, all in one dashboard.
Finance Admin
Subscription & Payment Management
View every investor subscription by plan type, review payment transactions, and run revenue analysis and financial reports across the platform.
Finance Admin
Expert Document Verification
Review the documents and LinkedIn profile submitted by investors applying to become an Expert, then approve or reject the application. Approved verifications can no longer be cancelled.
Finance Admin
Admin Features
User Account Management
View, search, create, suspend, and delete user accounts. Manage user profiles and access levels across Basic, Premium, Expert, Finance Admin, and Admin roles.
Admin
Content Moderation
Monitor, edit, and delete forum posts and comments, moderate platform reviews, and manage investment guidance articles, adding, editing, or removing educational content to maintain platform accuracy.
Admin
Expert Application Review
Review investors who have applied to become experts alongside their trading track record, before the application moves to Finance Admin for document verification.
Admin
Notification Management
Send platform-wide or targeted notifications to users, and manage the notification templates and history behind them.
Admin
Admin Dashboard
Centralized admin portal with sidebar navigation for user management, content control, expert application review, and system monitoring, all accessible from a single interface.
